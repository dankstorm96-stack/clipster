## 1. Market (market-research)

**Primary audience (about 80% of spend): businesses with no website**
- Instagram and WhatsApp sellers taking orders by DM
- Home businesses: bakers, clothing, jewellery, candles, skincare, art
- Local shops and boutiques that only sell to walk-in customers
- Offline brands that want to sell beyond their city

**Secondary audience (about 20% of spend): businesses that need a redesign**
- Stores that are slow, outdated, bad on phones, or get visitors but few orders

### Pain in their own words
- "DM for price" / "Price?": answering the same question 50 times a day
- "Do you have a website?": customers don't trust brands that only exist on Instagram
- "I don't understand Shopify, domains, payment gateways…"
- "My last web guy disappeared halfway through."
- "If I stop posting, sales stop."
- "I can only sell to people near my shop."

### What keeps them up at night
- "I replied to DMs all day and sold three things."
- "What if my Instagram gets hacked or banned?"
- "Everyone else looks more professional than me."

## 3. The Offer: "The 14-Day Store Launch" (value-perception, bonus-stack)

**Core:** a complete online store, designed and built for you.

| Objection | Bonus that kills it |
|---|---|
| "I don't know tech" | Full setup: domain, payments, shipping and order emails, all connected |
| "My product pages will look weak" | We write the product descriptions for your first 20 products ⚠️ |
| "Will anyone even visit?" | Launch Kit: 10 post and reel templates, plus a launch message for your existing followers and customers |
| "I won't know how to run it" | A 10-minute "run your store" video and 30 days of support after launch ⚠️ |
| "What if it looks cheap?" | Design revisions until you're happy, before it goes live |

**Guarantee:** live in 14 days or you don't pay ⚠️ (time-based risk reversal).
**Redesign guarantee:** if the new site doesn't convert better than the old one in 60 days, we keep working free ⚠️.
**Payment terms:** 50% upfront. Then 1 month of unlimited revisions, and the second 50% is paid only once the customer is satisfied.
**Urgency:** "We only launch X stores a month" ⚠️. Use this only if it's true.

## 4. Objections → Belief Shifts (objection-destroyer)

| They believe | Shift them to |
|---|---|
| "It's too expensive" | You're already paying for not having one: lost orders and lost hours in DMs |
| "I'm too small for a website" | A website is what makes small brands look big |
| "Instagram is enough" | You don't own Instagram. One ban or algorithm change and your sales hit zero |
| "I'll do it myself later" | DIY takes months and looks DIY. Every month without a store is sales lost |
| "Tech is too hard" | You'll never touch the tech. Send photos, then approve |
| "Designers disappear" | You only pay the second half once you're happy, after a month of unlimited changes |

## 8. Production + Testing Plan

**Production**
- Vertical 9:16 video. Talking head or selfie style beats polished. Captions burned in.
- The hook should be on screen as text in the first frame. Most viewers watch with sound off.
- Show real client stores in the 10–20s section as soon as you have them. Proof beats copy.

**Testing**
1. Launch all 5 scripts with equal small budgets (about 80% on scripts 1–4, 20% on script 5).
2. After about 3–5 days, judge by **3-second view rate** (is the hook working?) and **cost per lead**.
3. Keep the top 1–2. Shoot 5 new opening lines for each winner with the body unchanged.
4. Refresh the opening every 2–3 weeks. Hooks wear out; the offer doesn't.
